NEON//BEAT Quick Guide

Play pads

Click or touch the 16 pads. The computer keys 1–4, Q–R, A–F, and Z–V mirror the grid.

Build a loop

Click a sequencer step once for a normal hit, twice for an accent, and again to switch it off.

Record live

Arm a track with R, press the global record button, start playback, then perform. Hits quantize to the closest step.

Change a track sound

Use its instrument menu, or drag any performance pad onto a track row to assign that sound and pitch.

Pattern variations

Slots A–D hold independent arrangements. Duplicate a slot, generate ideas, humanize velocity, or double 16 steps to 32.

Merge the beat

Bounce WAV renders the active pattern into one stereo audio file with track levels and panning.

808 & hip-hop lab

Open Hip-Hop or 808 Lab for punch kicks, trap hats, clean, distorted and sliding 808s, Reese bass, bells, brass, vocal texture and DJ cuts. Thump boosts the chest-frequency zone; Punch preserves drum attack.

Six visual themes

Ryobi Night, Miami Circuit, Solar Flare, Glacier Wave, Ultraviolet and Candy Frequency each replace the full palette, ambience, background effect and pad-hit animation.

Screen pet

Toggle the pet from the toolbar, customize one of six character bodies and seven dance moves, then drag it anywhere. It locks to every quarter-note pulse.

Visual descriptions

Turn on Describe mode, then hover or focus controls, pads, steps, tracks and modules for a plain-language explanation in the floating description bar.

Save projects

Save stores complete projects locally. Export and import project JSON from the Projects window for backup or sharing.

Shortcuts

Space play/stop · Shift+R record · M metronome · Ctrl/Cmd+S save · Ctrl/Cmd+E bounce · arrows change octave.
