NEON//BEAT Quick Guide

Play and assign pads

Tap a pad to preview it. Drag a colored sound identifier from the library, another pad, or a track onto any synth pad to replace its voice.

Paint a loop

Use Draw, Accent, or Erase, then drag across the channel rack. Right-click still cycles trigger probability.

Record live

Enable Auto Track, press Record, then perform. Each assigned pad sound creates or reuses a matching channel-rack lane and quantizes into the loop.

Preview and drag tracks

Tap a track’s colored identifier to audition it. Drag only that identifier to reorder the lane or copy its sound onto a synth pad.

Pattern variations

Slots A–D hold independent arrangements. Duplicate a slot, generate ideas, humanize velocity, or double 16 steps to 32.

Merge the beat

Bounce WAV renders the active pattern into one stereo audio file with track levels and panning.

808 & hip-hop lab

Open Hip-Hop or 808 Lab for punch kicks, trap hats, clean, distorted and sliding 808s, Reese bass, bells, brass, vocal texture and DJ cuts. Thump boosts the chest-frequency zone; Punch preserves drum attack.

Six interface styles

Dark, Light, Dim, Cyber, Matrix, and Tech provide practical production views without changing your audio or project data.

Screen pet

Toggle the pet from the toolbar, customize one of six character bodies and seven dance moves, then drag it anywhere. It locks to every quarter-note pulse.

Visual descriptions

Turn on Describe mode, then hover or focus controls, pads, steps, tracks and modules for a plain-language explanation in the floating description bar.

Save projects

Save stores complete projects locally. Export and import project JSON from the Projects window for backup or sharing.

Output guard

Safe Audio adds headroom, all wet effects now pass through the final limiter, and the ceiling control prevents accidental overload.

Mix translation

Use mono check, stereo width, rumble filtering, and the live peak monitor to make the loop translate better across speakers and headphones.

Pattern chain

Chain A→B or A→B→C→D to audition longer arrangements without manually switching pattern slots.

Shortcuts

Space play/stop · Shift+R record · M metronome · Ctrl/Cmd+S save · Ctrl/Cmd+E bounce · arrows change octave.
